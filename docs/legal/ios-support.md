# DayPage iOS 支持 / Support

DayPage 是无需账户的本地笔记应用。文字、照片、录音和附件保存在设备上；AI 对话和外部朗读需要自行配置服务，费用由相应服务商决定。

如需反馈问题，请在应用侧栏打开“反馈”，或前往 [DayPage GitHub Issues](https://github.com/getyak/daypage/issues)。请提供应用版本、iOS 版本、复现步骤和预期结果。问题通常公开可见，请勿附上私密笔记、API 密钥、登录资料或未经处理的个人截图。

隐私问题请参考 [iOS 隐私政策](ios-privacy.md)，并在反馈中说明具体疑问。请先导出备份，再进行卸载等可能删除本地数据的操作。

---

DayPage is a local notebook that needs no account. Text, photos, recordings and attachments stay in local files. AI chat and external read-aloud services require your configuration; provider charges may apply.

For help, open Feedback in the app sidebar or visit [DayPage GitHub Issues](https://github.com/getyak/daypage/issues). Include the app version, iOS version, steps to reproduce and expected result. Issues are generally public. Do not include private notes, API keys, sign-in details or screenshots containing personal information.

For privacy questions, read the [iOS privacy policy](ios-privacy.md) and describe the specific concern in your feedback. Export a backup before uninstalling or taking other actions that may remove local data.
